Next Transfer Privacy Policy

Effective Date: September 8, 2026

Developer: Planward Labs

Privacy & Support: next.transfer.app@gmail.com

1. Introduction

Next Transfer is a productivity and planning application designed to help returned missionaries organize tasks, events, goals, contacts, plans, and related information.

This Privacy Policy explains how Next Transfer accesses, uses, stores, and handles information when you use the current version of the application.

2. Local-First Design

Next Transfer is currently designed as a local-first application.

Planner information such as tasks, calendar events, goals, weekly plans, contacts, notes, preferences, and manually saved map locations is stored primarily in private application storage on your device.

The current version of Next Transfer does not provide an online user account, cloud synchronization, or a Planward Labs-operated cloud database.

3. Information You Provide

You may choose to enter information into Next Transfer, including:

  • tasks and planning information
  • calendar events
  • goals and activities
  • weekly plans
  • notes
  • contact information
  • an optional local profile or display name
  • manually selected map locations or coordinates
  • application preferences

This information is stored locally in the application's private database on your device.

The current version does not upload this planner database to a Planward Labs server.

4. Device Contacts

Next Transfer includes an optional feature that allows you to import selected contacts from your device.

If you choose to use this feature, Next Transfer may request permission to read your device contacts.

The import feature accesses contact information needed for the import process, including:

  • contact name
  • phone number
  • email address

The import process is initiated by you.

You choose which contacts to import into Next Transfer.

Imported contact information is stored locally inside Next Transfer.

Next Transfer does not modify or write to your device's original contacts.

You may continue using the application's manual contact and planning features without granting contact access.

5. Maps

Next Transfer includes map functionality using MapLibre and map data provided through OpenFreeMap.

The current version of Next Transfer does not request access to your device's GPS, precise location, approximate location, or background location.

Next Transfer does not automatically determine your current location.

Map pins are placed manually by the user.

When map content is displayed, your device connects to OpenFreeMap infrastructure to obtain map styles and map tiles.

As with ordinary internet communication, these requests may expose technical information such as the device's IP address, request headers, and requested map tiles to the map service provider.

Next Transfer does not receive or maintain OpenFreeMap's server logs.

According to OpenFreeMap's published privacy information, IP addresses are not normally retained in its regular server logs, although temporary logging may occur for security purposes.

The privacy practices of OpenFreeMap are governed by its own privacy policy: https://openfreemap.org/privacy/.

6. Manually Saved Locations

Next Transfer may allow you to manually place map pins associated with supported app records.

These coordinates are selected by you and stored locally in Next Transfer.

They are not derived from your device's GPS location.

If you explicitly choose to open a location in an external navigation application, the relevant coordinates may be passed to that external application through the Android operating system.

7. External Applications

Certain Next Transfer features allow you to open other applications installed on your device.

For example, you may choose to:

  • call a contact
  • open an SMS application
  • compose an email
  • open WhatsApp or another supported application
  • open a navigation application

These actions occur only when you initiate them.

Information required for the requested action may be passed to the external application you choose.

Next Transfer does not read your conversations and does not automatically record what happens after you leave Next Transfer.

The privacy practices of external applications are governed by their respective privacy policies.

8. Biometric Privacy Lock

Next Transfer offers an optional Privacy Lock feature that may use authentication provided by your device, including biometric authentication or device credentials.

Authentication is performed by the device operating system.

Next Transfer does not receive or store your fingerprint, facial recognition template, or other biometric information.

9. Analytics, Advertising and Tracking

The current version of Next Transfer does not include:

  • advertising SDKs
  • third-party advertising
  • advertising ID collection
  • analytics SDKs
  • behavioral analytics
  • third-party crash-reporting SDKs
  • advertising attribution SDKs

Next Transfer does not sell personal information.

10. Accounts and Cloud Sync

The current version of Next Transfer does not require or provide an online user account.

Cloud backup and synchronization through Planward Labs are not currently active.

Next Transfer may introduce optional account, online backup, synchronization, notification, or other connected functionality in future versions.

If future functionality changes how information is accessed, collected, transmitted, stored, used, or shared, this Privacy Policy and applicable app-store disclosures will be updated accordingly.

11. Notifications

The current version of Next Transfer does not send push notifications or scheduled application notifications.

If notification functionality is introduced in a future release, this Privacy Policy will be updated where necessary before or when that functionality becomes available.

12. Data Storage and Security

Next Transfer stores application information in private application storage on the user's device.

The current Next Transfer local database should not be considered fully encrypted or end-to-end encrypted.

Next Transfer may use Android platform security protections and the optional Privacy Lock feature to provide additional protection against unauthorized access.

Android application backup is disabled for the current Next Transfer application configuration.

No method of electronic storage or device security can guarantee absolute security.

13. Data Retention and Deletion

Because the current version of Next Transfer primarily stores information locally, information generally remains on the device according to the lifecycle of the applicable record.

Depending on the type of information, records may be:

  • deleted
  • archived
  • cancelled
  • merged
  • retained locally for historical functionality

For example, some planner records support permanent deletion, while other records may use archived, cancelled, merged, or deleted states.

Users can remove or modify information using the controls available for each supported feature.

Uninstalling Next Transfer normally removes the application's private local database from the device.

Android application backup is disabled for Next Transfer.

The current version does not maintain a Planward Labs-hosted cloud copy of the user's planner database.

14. Children's Privacy

Next Transfer is designed as a productivity and planning application for returned missionaries and is not specifically directed toward children.

The current application does not contain advertising or public social-network features.

15. Third-Party Services

The current version of Next Transfer may interact with third-party software, services, or applications, including:

  • MapLibre
  • OpenFreeMap
  • external applications explicitly opened by the user

Third parties may process information according to their own terms and privacy policies.

Next Transfer's use of a third-party service does not mean that Planward Labs controls that third party's independent privacy practices.

16. No Sale of Personal Information

Planward Labs does not sell personal information collected through the current version of Next Transfer.

The current version also does not operate an advertising or data-broker business based on information stored in Next Transfer.

17. Future Features

Next Transfer is under active development.

Future versions may introduce functionality such as online accounts, cloud backup, synchronization, notifications, crash reporting, analytics, or other connected services.

Such functionality must not be interpreted as active merely because it is mentioned here.

When a future feature materially changes Next Transfer's data practices, Planward Labs intends to update this Privacy Policy and applicable Google Play disclosures to reflect the functionality actually shipped.

18. Changes to This Privacy Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy when Next Transfer's features, technologies, services, or legal requirements change.

When material changes are made, the Effective Date at the top of this Privacy Policy will be updated.

Users are encouraged to review this page periodically.
